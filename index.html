Research

※ 悬停语句可查看引文 · Hover to reveal citations

I want to build intelligent systems that perceive the physical world, reason about what they see, and act to change it. My long-term goal is to close the loop from sensing to understanding to interaction — making machines that not only model the world, but truly live in it.

路漫漫其修远兮，吾将上下而求索。屈原《离骚》

▸ Perceive. Helping machines see structure beneath appearance — geometry, pose, and semantics — from images, videos, and point clouds, with a current focus on open-vocabulary 3D scene understanding.

格物致知，诚意正心。《礼记·大学》

▸ Reason. Studying how large models can reason over structured knowledge — graphs, programs, and tools — so that their chain of thought is grounded, verifiable, and efficient.

学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。《论语·为政》

▸ Act. Closing the gap between pixels and actions through embodied agents and robot learning, letting systems improve through interaction with the real world rather than static datasets.

知者行之始，行者知之成。王阳明《传习录》
