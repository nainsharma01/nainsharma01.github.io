Research

I want to teach generative models the physics of shape — so the geometry they create is controllable by intent, constrained by physics, and ready for the real world. Rather than shapes that merely look right, my goal is generation that is right: faithful to forces, valid under constraints, and editable by design.

形而上者谓之道，形而下者谓之器。《周易·系辞上》

▸ Represent. Studying structured 3D representations — meshes, B-Reps, and CAD construction sequences — that keep generated geometry compact, editable, and faithful to engineering intent.

圣人立象以尽意。《周易·系辞上》

▸ Generate. Developing controllable generative frameworks, such as diffusion and autoregressive models, driven by explicit conditions — sketches, parameters, and constraints — and guided by physical priors such as forces, materials, and stability.

生生之谓易。《周易·系辞上》

▸ Realize. Closing the loop between generation and the physical world by validating generated geometry with physics simulation and manufacturability analysis, so that what is designed can actually be made.
